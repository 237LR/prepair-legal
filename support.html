Getting Started

Creating Your Account

  1. Download Prepair from the Apple App Store
  2. Open the app and tap "Get Started"
  3. Enter your email address and create a password
  4. Complete the onboarding questionnaire
  5. Start practicing interviews!

Setting Up Your Profile

A complete profile helps us personalize your experience:

  • Basic Info: Name, current role, company
  • Experience: Years of experience, career level
  • Goals: Target role and industry
  • Skills: Technical and soft skills
  • History: Work experience and education

Go to Profile → Edit Profile to complete your information.

Your First Interview Practice

  1. Go to the Practice tab
  2. Choose an interview type (Smart Interview, Professional, Video, etc.)
  3. Select your target role and difficulty
  4. Grant microphone permission when prompted
  5. Answer the questions naturally
  6. Review your feedback and score

Account & Login Issues

Can't Log In?

Solution:

  • Verify your email and password are correct
  • Try the "Forgot Password" option
  • Check your spam folder for the password reset email
  • Ensure you have an active internet connection
  • Update to the latest version of the app

Forgot Your Password?

  1. Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Check your email for a password reset link
  4. Click the link and create a new password
  5. Log in with your new password

Want to Delete Your Account?

Steps:

  1. Go to Settings → Profile
  2. Scroll to the bottom
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion
Warning: This action is permanent. All your data, including interview history, will be deleted within 30 days and cannot be recovered.

Alternatively, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com to request account deletion.

To cancel or modify:

Requesting a Refund

Technical Problems

App Crashes or Freezes

Try these fixes:

  1. Force close the app and reopen it
  2. Restart your device
  3. Update to the latest version from the App Store
  4. Free up storage space on your device
  5. If problem persists, email us with device model and iOS version

Microphone Not Working

  1. Go to iOS Settings → Privacy → Microphone
  2. Find Prepair and toggle it ON
  3. Restart the app
  4. Test with a practice interview

Camera Not Working (Video Interviews)

  1. Go to iOS Settings → Privacy → Camera
  2. Find Prepair and toggle it ON
  3. Restart the app
  4. Try video interview mode again

Audio Playback Issues

  • Check device volume settings
  • Ensure Bluetooth devices aren't interfering
  • Try using headphones
  • Restart the app
  • Check that Silent/Ring switch is not on silent

Slow Performance

  • Close other apps running in the background
  • Free up device storage (need at least 1GB free)
  • Check internet connection speed
  • Update to the latest iOS version
  • Reinstall the app as a last resort

Feature Questions

Interview Types Explained

  • Smart Interview: Quick, conversational practice with instant feedback
  • Professional Interview: Formal interview with customizable difficulty
  • Video Interview: Practice with camera recording for body language feedback
  • Natural Interview: Casual, flowing conversation style

How Scoring Works

Your interview performance is scored based on:

  • Content Quality: Relevance, depth, and accuracy of answers
  • Communication: Clarity, structure (STAR method), and coherence
  • Confidence: Speech patterns, pace, filler word usage
  • Relevance: How well you addressed the question

Understanding Feedback

After each interview, you receive:

  • Overall Score: 0-100 rating of your performance
  • Key Strengths: What you did well
  • Areas for Improvement: Specific suggestions
  • Better Approach: Alternative answers for some questions

Career Coach Feature

Get personalized career advice:

  • Ask questions about career transitions, salary, networking, etc.
  • Toggle "Use My Profile Info" for personalized advice
  • Have back-and-forth conversations (up to 50 messages)
  • View past conversations in Recent Sessions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Prepair available on Android?

Currently, Prepair is only available for iOS devices. Android support is planned for the future.

Can I use Prepair offline?

Premium users can download interview questions for offline practice. However, scoring, feedback, and Career Coach require an internet connection.

How accurate is the interview feedback?

Our feedback is powered by advanced models. However, it should be used as a practice tool, not as definitive career advice. Always verify important information with qualified professionals.

Can I practice in languages other than English?

Yes! Prepair supports 30+ languages. Select your language in Settings → Preferences → Language.

Are my interview recordings stored?

Recordings are stored locally on your device. If you enable backup, they're securely stored in cloud storage. You can delete recordings anytime.

Will using Prepair guarantee I get a job?

No. Prepair is a practice tool to improve your interview skills. Job outcomes depend on many factors including qualifications, market conditions, and employer preferences.

Can I share my account with others?

No. Each user must have their own account. Account sharing violates our Terms of Service and may result in suspension.

How do I report a bug?

Email karoljaworsky@gmail.com with:

  • Description of the problem
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Device model and iOS version
  • Screenshots if applicable
