5.1 Viewing Your Subscription

To view your active subscription:

  1. Open Prepair app
  2. Go to Settings → Subscription
  3. Or go to iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions

5.2 Cancelling Your Subscription

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open iOS Settings
  2. Tap [Your Name] at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Prepair Premium
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"

After Cancellation:

  • You will retain premium access until the end of your current billing period
  • Your subscription will not renew
  • You will not receive a refund for the unused portion of your subscription
  • After expiration, you will revert to the free tier

5.3 Reactivating Subscription

You can reactivate a cancelled subscription anytime before it expires:

  • Follow the cancellation steps above
  • Tap "Renew Subscription" instead
  • Your subscription will continue at the next billing cycle
