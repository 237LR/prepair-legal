2. What Pro Includes and Its Fair-Use Limits

2.1 Pro Features

  • Live interviews: full-length, real-time spoken interviews with an AI interviewer, with every persona and company available
  • Rapid fire: unlimited single-question practice with a score and a note on each answer
  • Debriefs and feedback: a full written debrief after every Live interview
  • Salary negotiation simulator
  • Analytics: progress tracking across your sessions

2.2 Live Minute Allowance

Every Live interview minute is billed to us by our voice provider, so Pro includes a generous but finite allowance rather than unlimited use:

  • 150 Live minutes per week. The week runs Monday to Sunday and the allowance resets every Monday at 00:00 local time. Unused minutes do not roll over.
  • At most 60 Live minutes per calendar day. This keeps the weekly allowance from being used up in one sitting.
  • Only the time you spend in a connected Live interview counts. A Live interview needs at least 3 minutes of allowance to start.
  • The allowance is per account, not per device.
  • The remaining allowance is shown in the App before you start a Live interview.

We may adjust these limits in the future; any reduction will be announced in advance under section 17 and will not apply to a yearly period you have already paid for.

2.3 Live Minute Packs

  • Minute packs are consumable, one-time purchases. They are not subscriptions and do not renew.
  • Purchased minutes never expire and are added to your account immediately after purchase.
  • Purchased minutes are used only after the current week's included allowance is used up, and remain subject to the 60-minutes-per-day limit.
  • Purchased minutes can only be used with an active Pro subscription. If your subscription ends, unused purchased minutes are kept and become usable again when you resubscribe.
  • Purchased minutes are tied to your account and cannot be transferred, exchanged for cash, or refunded by Prepair (see section 7).
