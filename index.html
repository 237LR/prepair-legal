Key Information

🌍 GDPR & EU Compliance

We comply with GDPR and EU data protection regulations. EU users have specific rights including right to access, rectification, erasure, and data portability. See our Privacy Policy for details.

🇺🇸 CCPA Compliance

California residents have additional privacy rights under CCPA, including right to know, right to delete, and right to opt-out. We do not sell personal information.

🍎 Apple App Store

Prepair is distributed through the Apple App Store. All purchases, subscriptions, and refunds are subject to Apple's policies and terms.

🤖 Third-Party AI Services

We use Grok AI, AssemblyAI, Google Gemini TTS, and Google Nano Banana CV to power our features. Your data is shared with these services as needed to provide functionality.

🔐 Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS), secure authentication (Supabase), and follow best practices to protect your data.

⚖️ Dispute Resolution

Disputes are resolved through binding arbitration rather than court, except for small claims. Class action waiver applies. See Terms of Service for full details.
